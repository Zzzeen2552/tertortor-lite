## 1. Core look

A cinematic, stylized 3D world with "heightened realism": faces are expressive and slightly Pixar-like (larger eyes, soft planes, gentle caricature), while everything around them is physically tactile, weathered and period-correct. Color is muted and filmic, with soft haze and shallow depth of field. The mood is gritty but hopeful, a documentary told with the warmth of an animated feature.

## 2. Palette

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Base | Sepia brown, dusty ochre, warm tan, faded khaki, worn grey-blue cloth |
| Sky and shadow | Faded teal-blue sky with soft white cumulus; cool blue-grey in shadow |
| Interiors | Warm tungsten amber against peeling teal-blue walls |
| Night | Deep blue hour with orange lantern and bulb bokeh |
| Accents (one or two per frame) | Roasting-machine red, a red shirt, burnt-orange flowers, a green plant, brass |
| Success moments | Slightly cleaner, brighter blues and reds (ocean, containers, shiny steel) |

Overall grade: warm and desaturated at the bottom of the story, gradually cleaner and bluer as the hero rises.

## 3. Light

- **Day, hardship:** soft overcast or hazy sun, long low-contrast shadows, dust in the air, cloud-filled teal sky.
- **Day, hope:** golden-hour side light on cobblestones and faces.
- **Interior:** hanging tungsten bulbs, window light shafts, steel highlights on machines, warm falloff to dark corners.
- **Night market:** blue hour with hanging paper lanterns, wet street reflections, strong orange bokeh.
- Always soft and cinematic; avoid hard studio light and neon.

## 4. Materials and rendering

- **Fabric:** burlap sacks with printed stencil marks, frayed hems, rolled sleeves, patched trousers, worn linen and cotton, wool vests, tweed jackets.
- **Surfaces:** wet or dry cobblestone, cracked and stained plaster, peeling blue paint, rusted steel hulls and mooring parts, weathered wood piers.
- **Objects:** wicker baskets, shoulder poles (pikulan), brass and chrome machines, glass jars, paper coffee bags, ledgers, enamel cups, kerosene and filament lamps.
- **Skin and hair:** smooth stylized skin with freckles, dirt smudges or sweat for the poor; spiky or messy dark hair on the boy; neat grey hair, moustache and spectacles on older men.
- **Depth of field:** shallow; subject sharp, background and secondary figures softly blurred, often with a distant person or two for scale.
- **Finish:** subtle film grain, gentle vignette, slight lens softness at edges.

## 5. Characters and expressions

**Heritage first.** Begin every character sentence with heritage, because it controls face, skin, clothing and setting: *Javanese, Sundanese, Minang, Bugis, Batak, Madurese, Betawi, Peranakan Chinese-Indonesian, Totok Chinese-Indonesian, Arab-Indonesian, Indo-European*. Appearance anchors (use light, respectful, individual details; never exaggerate):

| Heritage | Anchors |
|---|---|
| Javanese, Sundanese, Betawi and most island-Southeast-Asian groups | warm tawny to medium-brown skin, dark brown eyes, thick black hair, softly rounded face, wide nose bridge, full lips |
| Minang, Batak, Bugis, Madurese | same warm brown range with slightly stronger cheekbones or jaw; regional clothing carries identity |
| Peranakan Chinese-Indonesian | lighter warm-beige to golden skin, softly almond-shaped dark eyes, straight black hair; Indonesian clothing and surroundings (kebaya encim, sarong, cotton jackets), local speech and neighbors |
| Arab-Indonesian | olive-brown skin, thick brows, dark wavy hair, peci or white cap |

For Chinese-Indonesian heroes, keep every other cue Indonesian (tropical street, mixed neighbors, local dress), so they read as people of this land.

**Protagonist ladder** (reuse the same sentence in every prompt of that age; adapt details and heritage to the story):

| Age | Fixed description |
|---|---|
| Boy, 10–13 | [heritage], skinny, tousled spiky black hair, large dark eyes, freckles and dirt smudges, loose faded grey-blue open shirt over a stained cream tee, rope belt or brown leather belt, a canvas strap across the chest, barefoot or in worn sandals |
| Young man, 18–25 | [heritage], lean, wavy dark hair parted loosely, linen shirt in sand khaki with rolled sleeves, a diagonal strap, sturdy but plain trousers, sinewy forearms |
| Mature man, 35–50 | [heritage], short neat hair, white shirt with rolled sleeves and a plain vest, darker trousers, composed expression |
| Elder, 60+ | [heritage], grey hair, rounded spectacles optional, tailored dark suit or batik shirt, calm eyes |

Choose or alter details once, then keep them fixed. For recurring characters, generate a character sheet first (see `nano-banana-guide.md`).

**Supporting archetypes** (invented, never real people; each gets a heritage): Javanese street vendor with shoulder pole and a weathered smiling face; wise old Chinese-Indonesian craftsman with grey beard and leather apron; Dutch merchant in a tweed jacket and bow tie (one among many; most people on the street are Indonesian); Peranakan shopkeeper with round glasses; clerk behind a bank counter; mixed dockworkers; laughing customers of several backgrounds around a table.

**Expression vocabulary:** tired, worried with pinched brows, embarrassed, stubborn, hopeful, startled, relieved, proud, laughing with eyes crinkled, thoughtful. Describe the brows and eyes, not just the emotion word.

## 6. Framing and composition

- **16:9** is the default.
- **Hero portrait:** subject stands in the left or center third, slightly low camera, shop or street receding behind with one landmark (clock tower, signboard, ship masts).
- **Walking or cycling:** medium shot from the front at chest height, shallow depth of field, street leading lines.
- **Hands and objects:** extreme close-up with creamy background; ledger, price tag, dials, coffee beans.
- **Wide scale shots:** harbor with several ships and masts, container ship on blue ocean, crowded market.
- **Over-the-shoulder** for mentor or negotiation scenes.
- Leave negative space in the sky or wall where subtitles and titles can sit.

## 7. Recurring motifs

| Motif | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Harbor, steamships, masts | Trade, distance, arrival |
| Cobblestone streets | Colonial era, wear and journey |
| Shoulder poles, bicycles with baskets | Humble distribution |
| Roasting machine or main trade tool | The hero's craft |
| Book or ledger | Self-teaching, discipline |
| Hanging bulb over a bed or table | Late-night study |
| Price tag or banknote stack | The cost of a dream |
| Handshake | The turning point |
| Laughing table with cups | Community, success |
| Container ship on open ocean | Empire at scale |

Optional channel motif: a small bamboo raft or riverbank in the opening and closing frames, echoing the proverb in the name.

## 8. Shot library

1. **Humble origin portrait:** boy standing in an old street, worried eyes, colonial building and clock tower behind, signage on a shop.
2. **Pier with sacks:** barefoot child carrying heavy sacks, harbor with masted ships and steamers behind, cloud-filled sky.
3. **Vendor on the move:** old man with shoulder pole baskets walking a shophouse street, smiling, shallow depth of field.
4. **Cycling distribution:** young man on a bicycle with a basket of paper bags, palm trees and shophouses behind.
5. **Night market:** same figure at blue hour with orange lanterns, wet street, bokeh.
6. **Self-study:** face lit by a bare bulb, reading a thick technical book on a peeling blue wall, worried brows.
7. **Hands on machine:** close-up of hands placing a price tag on a steel roasting drum.
8. **The deal:** foreign merchant with moustache and bow tie, arms crossed or offering a handshake, workshop behind.
9. **Rejection at the bank:** worried small man with round glasses, blurred clerks and brass counter behind.
10. **Mentor in the workshop:** old craftsman with grey beard laughing and explaining, child seen from behind.
11. **The first customers:** men laughing around a table with coffee cups and a paper bag at center.
12. **Machine hero shot:** low-angle, steel and red paint with window light.
13. **Scale reveal:** huge container ship on blue open sea, crisp light.
14. **Time-jump:** the same street decades later, cleaner and busier, same landmark.

## 9. Drift to avoid

- Over-polished plastic skin, uncanny photorealism, or flat cartoon outlines.
- Neon teal-orange blockbuster grade or heavy HDR.
- Clean spotless clothes on the struggling hero.
- Generic "old-timey" sepia with no color accents.
- Modern signage, LEDs, plastic packaging, brand logos.
- Real people's likenesses or names; ethnic caricature.
- Generic East Asian or Western faces and costumes, European-town architecture, bare temperate trees, snow-like skies; Indonesians must look Indonesian and the place must feel tropical.
- Cluttered backgrounds that fight the subject.
