# Babad Tanah Jawa style bible

Extracted from three reference artworks: a vertical drawing of two crumbling red-brick gate pillars framing a distant brick shrine with a pale coconut palm behind; a drawing of a woman standing in a weathered brick gateway beneath a tiered thatched roof; and a charcoal-and-pastel drawing of two women at the steps of a brick wall with a tiered roof, one carrying an offering bowl on her head. All three are signed and dated Bali, 1938–1941, in the tradition of Indies-era travel drawing. Use them for medium and mood. Do not name the artist in prompts and do not reproduce their compositions.

## 1. Core look

A hand-drawn sketchbook plate: charcoal and graphite drawn on warm toned paper, with colored pastel and a thin watercolor wash. The forms are old, eroded and tender: red-brick gates, moss, palms and tiered roofs, with a quiet figure placed in the architecture. The image feels like a memory of a place, not a record: detail concentrates in the middle and dissolves toward the paper edge.

## 2. Medium and rendering

- **Support:** warm buff, tan or parchment paper with visible tooth and slight mottling; the paper tone shows through everywhere and is left bare at the borders.
- **Line and shade:** loose charcoal and graphite hatching; brick courses built from many short strokes; soft smudged tonal gradients (stumped charcoal) in shadow areas.
- **Color:** colored pastel or chalk rubbed in thin layers; light transparent watercolor wash in the sky and on moss; tiny pure-pigment touches at focal points.
- **Foliage:** scribbled, airy marks; coconut palm fronds in pale silvery strokes against a darker sky; banyan roots as long vertical strokes.
- **Texture:** rough, grainy, unfinished; no clean vector edges; no gloss; no digital glow.
- **Finish:** looks like a scan of an original drawing: slight paper edge, soft pigment variations.

## 3. Palette

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Paper | Warm buff, sand, light tan |
| Structure | Charcoal black-brown, graphite grey, sepia |
| Brick | Terracotta, burnt sienna, brick red with dusty rose highlights |
| Sky | Pale grey with a faint dusty-rose or peach wash |
| Vegetation | Sage green, olive, pale yellow-green moss, silvery-white palm |
| Accents (one or two) | Saffron or orange headcloth, green-gold and black kain, indigo cloth |

Overall: a warm monochrome with selective color. About 70–80% of the image stays grey-brown; color gathers on the brick and on one small human detail.

## 4. Light and atmosphere

- Diffuse overcast tropical light, soft and shadowless, with only gentle tonal shading.
- A faint warm glow near the horizon or through the gateway.
- Mist or haze between layers; distant trees nearly erased into paper tone.
- No hard sun shafts, no rim light, no glow effects.
- For legend scenes: heavier mist, lower contrast, washes bleeding past the lines.

## 5. Composition

- Vertical plates are the reference format; landscape works by placing gate halves or trees left and right.
- **Frames inside frames:** a gate, an archway or two broken pillars frame the subject.
- **Layers:** foreground broken stones and weeds, middle gate or figure, background tiered roof and foliage fading into paper.
- Central vertical axis with a slightly off-center figure.
- Large calm areas of empty paper; leave room for title text.
- Quiet, still, nothing dramatic: the drama is time and ruin.

## 6. Figures

- Small, upright, slow; standing in a threshold, climbing steps, resting against brick, carrying a bowl or basket.
- Faces small and softly shaded, head tilted, turned away or lowered; never portraiture.
- **Clothing (default covered):** kain wrapped at the waist with a sash, a kemben or fitted bodice, shoulder cloth (selendang or sampur), headcloth or simple bun; men with kain, shoulder cloth and iket or udeng. Fabrics drawn with loose pattern marks: checks, diagonals, soft stripes.
- Posture shows mood: bowed, waiting, offering, watching.

## 7. Recurring motifs

| Motif | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Split gate (candi bentar) and roofed gate (paduraksa) of red brick | Threshold between worlds and eras |
| Tiered roofs (meru, tumpang) rising from trees | Sacred center, continuity |
| Coconut palms, banyan, frangipani | Tropical Java and Bali |
| Moss and weeds on broken steps | Time and decay |
| Offering bowl, water jar, basket | Daily devotion and labor |
| Weathered guardian statues | Memory of older faiths |
| Pools, bathing basins with steps | Purification, court life |
| Lontar leaves and stylus, oil lamp | The chronicle itself |
| Keris, spear, banners | Power, rarely drawn in action |
| Mist over a river, mountain or south sea | Legend |

## 8. Shot library

1. **Gate frame:** two broken brick gate halves frame a path and a distant shrine.
2. **Tower in trees:** a tiered roof rising over foliage, a gate pillar in the foreground.
3. **Figure in the gateway:** a lone figure leaning or standing at the threshold.
4. **Offering bearer:** a figure carrying a bowl on the head up worn steps.
5. **Pool steps:** a bathing basin with brick steps and a jar, no one bathing.
6. **Pendopo interior:** carved wooden pillars, mats, a seated group from behind.
7. **Court audience:** a ruler on a dais, courtiers kneeling, drawn from the back of the hall.
8. **Procession:** a line of small figures on a path with banners and umbrellas.
9. **Manuscript close-up:** hands writing on lontar leaves by an oil lamp.
10. **Mountain and mist:** Merapi, Lawu or Semeru as a faint wash above a village and rice fields.
11. **South sea at dusk:** surf and mist, a veiled figure far off (legend).
12. **Forest clearing:** founding of a settlement, axes and fires, brick pile.
13. **River crossing:** bamboo raft with two figures on a wide misty river (legend).
14. **Early mosque:** timber mosque with a tiered roof and an ablution pool, figures from behind.
15. **Aftermath:** shields, spears and banners laid on a field at dawn, no bodies.
16. **Time-jump:** the same gate whole, then ruined, in two plates.

## 9. Bali vs Java note

The reference works show Balinese temples, which preserve Majapahit-era forms (split gates, red brick, tiered roofs), so they are a good proxy for the mood of old Java. For Java specifically, name the real forms in the era sentence: Trowulan red-brick gates (Wringin Lawang, Bajang Ratu), Segaran pool, the timber Great Mosque of Demak, brick walls and banyan courts of Kotagede, joglo pendopo halls. Avoid Balinese-specific ornament (heavily carved candi bentar, Balinese dress and ceremonies) unless the story is set in Bali.

## 10. Drift to avoid

- Photoreal, glossy 3D, game-engine render, sharp digital painting with rim light, neon, saturated rainbow colors.
- Clean vector outlines, flat cartoon, anime, comic ink.
- Added signature, handwriting, captions, dates or fake artist marks.
- Heavy dramatic lighting, sunbeams, lens flare.
- Bare-chested default, overly sexualized poses, orientalist exoticism, caricature.
- Domes, minarets or concrete in Majapahit or Demak scenes; European buildings in Java before the colonial era.
- Real people's likenesses or named royals, depictions of the Prophet ﷺ, Wali faces.
