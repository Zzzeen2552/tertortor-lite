# Story beats to shots

The Babad Tanah Jawi is a collection of Javanese court chronicles, compiled and revised over time, with several versions that differ. It moves from mythic genealogy through Majapahit, Demak, Pajang and Mataram and into later Mataram history. It mixes myth, legend and dynastic politics, so a video can say "menurut Babad…" and keep legend and history visibly different. This is an outline for shot planning, not a historical authority: verify dates, names and events against scholarly sources.

## 2. Mapping script sentences to shots

- One concrete noun or place per image: choose the most drawable element of each sentence ("he cleared the forest" → a clearing with axes and a pile of bricks, not "the birth of a kingdom").
- For a place name, show its architecture; for a ruler, show the dais and courtiers from behind; for a moral or spiritual idea, show a gateway, a stairway or a tiered roof.
- For a crowd or army, draw a long line of small figures on a path; for a defeat, an empty field.
- When the script names a real person, translate to an anonymous archetype of the same role; leave the name to the voice-over.
- When the script mentions a Wali or a prophet, follow the sacred-figure rules in SKILL.md: no faces, no prophets.

## 3. Legend vs history in practice

| Cue | History | Legend |
|---|---|---|
| Edges | Crisper, brick courses drawn | Dissolving into paper tone |
| Mist | Light haze | Heavy fog, washes bleeding |
| Contrast | Medium | Low |
| Detail | Architectural | Few forms, one focal gesture |
| Color | Terracotta and sage | Greyed, one cool accent (blue-green) |
| Figures | Standing in architecture | Small, distant, veiled or turned away |

Say in the prompt: "dreamlike, heavy mist, edges bleed into bare paper" for legend; "carefully drawn brick courses" for history.

## 4. Pacing and variety

- Alternate gate frames, towers, figures and landscapes; never three similar compositions in a row.
- Insert an empty-place plate (no figures) every 4–6 images for breathing room.
- Keep a consistent paper tone across a batch by repeating the same sentence for paper and medium.

## 5. Transitions

- **Time-jump:** the same gate whole and then ruined, with identical framing.
- **Match cut:** a hand on lontar → a hand on a pillar → a hand on a keris hilt.
- **Gate to gate:** a Majapahit split gate → a Demak mosque doorway → a Mataram kraton gate, each framing a path.

## 6. Thumbnail frames

Use a strong gate frame with a lone figure and plenty of empty paper in one third for title text. Accent color on one small element (headcloth or brick glow). For a legend topic, use the mist grammar with a single veiled silhouette.
